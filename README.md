# Crystal Parfait Collective

You are a world-class Product Designer, Brand Strategist, UI/UX Designer and Senior Full Stack Engineer.

Your mission is to build a premium e-commerce website for **Crystal Love International**, the home of **Crystal Exotic Parfaits**.

This is not just another food ordering website.

It should feel like a luxury food brand similar to Apple, Häagen-Dazs, Magnolia Bakery and Starbucks.

The attached images are the ACTUAL brand assets.

Use them throughout the website.

• Product photography

• Founder photography

• Packaging

• Colors inspired by the labels

DO NOT use stock parfait images unless absolutely necessary.
